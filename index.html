Our Story

A Passion Kindled by Fire

Ember & Gold began in 1998 as a tiny family kitchen with one wood-fired oven and a belief that great food brings people together. Over twenty-five years later, that belief still guides every plate we serve.

We partner with local farmers and artisans, cooking with the seasons and letting honest ingredients speak. Every dish is a love letter to craft, warmth, and hospitality.
